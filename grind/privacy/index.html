Grind privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-10.

Grind uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What it accesses

With your consent, Grind uploads videos and edits video metadata on your YouTube channel, and reads that channel's analytics (views, watch time, subscribers).

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to publish your videos and report how they perform to you. OAuth tokens and analytics are stored on the operator's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

You can revoke Grind's access at any time at Google Account permissions. Stored tokens for your account are then useless, and are deleted on request.
